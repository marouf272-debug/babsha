## 4. Auction finding

400 four-player games: one Medium player who never buys at list price and only buys at auction, against three normal Medium players, rotating seats. 400 reached a winner.

The decliner won 21.3% ±4.0 of games. A neutral strategy would win about 25%.

Because an auction opens at ৳0 (plan 2.3, D-43) and the player who declined may bid (D-33), declining at list price and buying at auction for less pays off when other players do not bid the price up. Human players may bid more than these computer players do.
